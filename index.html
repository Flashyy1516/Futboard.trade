What is FutBoard?

FutBoard is a software platform for traders who distribute their trading posts across multiple communities. It lets you create a trade once and send it to selected destinations from a single workflow.

Who is FutBoard for?

FutBoard is designed for traders, tipsters and community operators who publish trading content across multiple servers.

Is FutBoard a trading bot?

No. FutBoard does not identify or execute trades. It handles the workflow involved in distributing trading content.

Can I select which servers receive each post?

Yes. Each trade can be distributed only to the communities or channels you choose.

Can I create different trade formats?

The interface supports reusable formats for different types of posts such as buys, sells and investments.

Does FutBoard guarantee profit?

No. FutBoard is a workflow and content distribution platform. It does not guarantee trading performance.
